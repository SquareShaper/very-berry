# Very Berry Mod

## Features
Adds 3 new berries to the game - the Rimeberry, the Fireshine Berry and the Thornberry. 
Adds 3* new foods to the game - the Rimeberry Muffin, the Fireshine Berry Juice and different types of Thornberry Chips. 
Adds 1 new potion effect to the game - the Thorny effect, making enemies take damage when hurting you. 
  
# Berries
## Rimeberry
Can be found in snowy biomes, like Jagged Peaks and Snowy Slopes. The berries grow on a bush like Sweet Berries, but the bush can only grow on snow blocks! Rimeberries give a very short Resistance buff when consumed. 

<img src="https://github.com/SquareShaper/very-berry/blob/master/PageAssets/loneBerry.png?raw=true" alt="A screenshot showing the Rimeberry Bush, growing in a snowy biome">

## Fireshine Berry
Can be found in the Nether Wastes biome, hanging from the ceiling. These hanging plants can be climbed and berry-bearing parts of the plant can be right-clicked to harvest Fireshine Berries! They provide as much hunger and saturation as Sweet Berries, but also give you a short burst of Fire Resistance, making your Nether journey a bit more safe! 

<img src="https://github.com/SquareShaper/very-berry/blob/master/PageAssets/FireshineBerryGrowth.png?raw=true" alt="A screenshot showing the Fireshine Berry Growth, hanging down from the Nether ceiling">

## Thornberry
Can be found growing on cacti in the desert. Right-clicking a cactus branch bearing a full-grown Thornberry will yield a delicious prickly berry! While they don't give much nutrition, they grant you a new effect making enemies think twice about striking you! 

<img src="https://github.com/SquareShaper/very-berry/blob/master/PageAssets/LonelyThornberryBranch.png?raw=true" alt="A screenshot showing a Thornberry Branch carrying a Thornberry, growing from a cactus in the desert">

# Foods

## Rimeberry Muffins
These muffins are quite satiating, for a fruity snack! They also provide you with a long lasting Resistance buff. 
Made with 2 pieces of Wheat, 2 Rimeberries and a Bucket of Milk. (You get the Bucket back, don't worry!)

<img src="https://github.com/SquareShaper/very-berry/blob/master/PageAssets/RimeberryMuffinRecipe_cropped.png?raw=true" alt="The crafting recipe for the Rimeberry Muffin">

## Fireshine Berry Juice
Juicing these berries not only increases their nutritional value, but also prolongs the Fire Resistance effect they give!
Made with 4 Fireshine Berries, some Sugar and a Glass Bottle to drink it from. 

<img src="https://github.com/SquareShaper/very-berry/blob/master/PageAssets/FireshineBerryJuiceRecipe_cropped.png?raw=true" alt="The crafting recipe for the Fireshine Berry Juice">

## Thornberry Chips
If you go through the process of slicing and baking you get a (not so) nutritious snack, that gives you a prolonged Thorny effect. They can be combined into bunches of 4, and even bundled for a treat that lasts!
Slicing a Thornberry yields 4 slices, which can then be smoked or baked at a campfire to make Thornberry Chips. These can be bunched together in packs of 4, and finally bundled up to store your greasy chips more conveniently.
